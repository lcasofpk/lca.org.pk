Advocacy & Legal Action

Serving as a constitutional watchdog for local democracy in Pakistan.

Historic Legal Milestones

2010–2015

Suspension Advocacy & Supreme Court Victory

Sustained litigation and advocacy led to a landmark Supreme Court of Pakistan ruling that paved the road for countrywide local government elections in 2015.

2019

Punjab Local Bodies Writ Petitions

ADLG and member associations (including LCAP) filed constitutional petitions in the Lahore High Court and Supreme Court challenging the premature dissolution of elected local bodies in Punjab.

Legislative Reform Agenda

Constitutional Protections

Guaranteeing fixed council terms and preventing arbitrary provincial dissolutions.

Fiscal Autonomy

Direct revenue allocation via regular Provincial Finance Commission (PFC) awards.

Service Autonomy

Keeping primary healthcare, education, municipal services, and water sanitation under direct local council control.
